# SceneChain ethics review application package

Status: ready for investigator and institutional completion; not an ethics approval.

## Project summary

SceneChain compares manual password login with direct and observation-shielded graphical login. Participants use only study credentials. The study measures completion time, success, delayed retention, workload, accessibility barriers, and complete-chain observer success. It does not protect real assets or request real passwords.

## Participants and recruitment

Recruit up to 90 adults to retain 72 analyzable participants. Exclude minors, pilot participants, investigators, and people unable to provide informed consent. Recruitment text must avoid coercion and state compensation, duration, eligibility, and voluntary withdrawal. Accessibility exclusions and alternative-path use are reported transparently.

## Procedures

Participants complete the three counterbalanced conditions, standardized practice, timed trials, questionnaires, and the declared follow-up. Observation assignments never target a participant's real credential. Direct mode is described as observable. Any recording requires separate consent and materials not included in the standard implementation.

## Risk assessment

Risk is no greater than ordinary low-stakes computer interaction, with possible frustration, fatigue, accessibility difficulty, or privacy concern. Mitigations include breaks, withdrawal, study-only warnings, no valuable resources, no real passwords, data minimisation, pseudonymisation, encryption, rate limiting, restricted export, and deletion.

## External submission

The investigator must use the committee responsible for their institution/faculty. TU Berlin committees state that participant information, instructions, and consent materials accompany the full application and advise submission well before collection. Insert the issued reference and conditions in every participant-facing document. No repository change can substitute for that committee decision.
