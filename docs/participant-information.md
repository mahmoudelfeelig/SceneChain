## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to compare an ordinary study password with two presentations of an experimental graphical authenticator. SceneChain is a research prototype and protects no valuable account. Participation is voluntary and intended for adults aged 18 or older.

## What you will do

You will create study-only credentials, complete two successful practice logins and three measured logins in each assigned condition, answer workload questions, and may complete a controlled live observation task. Do not use any password that you use elsewhere. The initial session lasts `[insert pilot-derived duration]` minutes. Three short retention trials are due seven days later, with a requested return window of plus or minus two days.

## Data collected

The study records a random handle, versioned consent timestamp, assigned condition order, period, phase, trial number, success/failure/timeout, total and bounded per-stage durations, retry count, coarse viewport class, allowlisted browser family and input method, deviations, and aggregate cell/action frequencies. Credential verifiers and encrypted graphical metadata support authentication but are excluded from research exports. The study does not record names, email addresses, raw click coordinates, graphical secrets, typed passwords, IP addresses as research data, full user-agent strings, audio, video, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics.

## Risks and benefits

Risks include frustration, visual or motor difficulty, accidental disclosure of a study credential, and the privacy risk inherent in pseudonymous behavioral data. The prototype mitigates these risks through study-only accounts, a password alternative, encrypted credential material, restricted export, short retention, and withdrawal/deletion procedures. There is no guaranteed personal benefit.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

You may refuse or stop without penalty. Before anonymisation, you may request access or deletion using your study handle. Deleting data may no longer be possible after the re-linking information is destroyed and results are irreversibly aggregated.

## Complaints

Questions go to the investigator. Data-protection complaints may be directed to the institutional data-protection officer and the competent supervisory authority. Ethics concerns may be directed to the approving committee shown above.
