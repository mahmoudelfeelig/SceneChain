## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to compare an ordinary study password with two presentations
of an experimental graphical authenticator. SceneChain is a research prototype
and protects no valuable account. Participation is voluntary and limited to
adults aged 18 or older.

## What participation involves

You will create study-only credentials, complete practice and measured logins
in three assigned conditions, answer workload questions, and may complete a
controlled live observation task. Do not use a password used for any real
account. Three short retention trials are due seven days later, with a
requested return window of plus or minus two days.

The observation task is live and is not recorded. Introducing any recording
would require an approved protocol amendment and separate opt-in consent.

## Data collected

The study records a random handle, consent version and timestamp, assigned
condition order, period, phase, trial number, success/failure/timeout, total and
bounded per-stage durations, retry count, coarse viewport class, allowlisted
browser family and input method, deviations, workload responses, and aggregate
cell/action frequencies.

Credential verifiers and encrypted graphical metadata support authentication
but are excluded from research exports. The study does not collect names,
email addresses, raw pointer coordinates, graphical secrets, typed passwords,
IP addresses as research data, full user-agent strings, audio, video,
advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or unrestricted free text.
Compensation records, if required, must be stored separately.

## Risks and benefits

Foreseeable risks are frustration, fatigue, visual or motor difficulty,
accidental disclosure of a study credential, and the privacy risk inherent in
pseudonymous behavioral data. Controls include study-only accounts, a password
alternative, breaks, withdrawal, encrypted credential material, restricted
export, short retention, and deletion rights. There is no guaranteed personal
benefit.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

You may refuse or stop without penalty. While data remains linked, you may
request access or permanent deletion using your study handle. Deletion may no
longer be possible after the re-linking material has been destroyed and results
have been irreversibly aggregated.

Questions go to the study contact. Data-protection complaints may be directed
to the named data-protection contact and competent supervisory authority.
Ethics concerns may be directed to the named committee.
