# SceneChain research plan

Status: frozen pending preregistration and written ethics/data-protection decisions.

SceneChain compares a manual study password with direct and shielded graphical authentication in a server-assigned six-sequence crossover. Each participant completes two successful practice logins, three measured immediate trials, raw NASA-TLX ratings, and one seven-day retention trial in each condition. The confirmatory sample is desktop/laptop users with viewports of at least 1024 by 600 CSS pixels.

The primary claim is non-inferiority of direct SceneChain to password completion time with a direct/password ratio margin of 1.25. Every issued measured trial is included; failures and timeouts receive the frozen 180-second ceiling. Success, shielded workload, delayed retention, enrollment, accessibility barriers, recovery, lockout behavior, aggregate choice concentration, and complete-chain observer success after exactly one opportunistic observation are secondary.

All 384 cells are selectable and no recommended locations are shown. Five scenes come from distinct families, recognition uses the full 48-scene public pool, the grid and crop are fixed, and four explicit actions are used. Shielded mode is not claimed to withstand repeated recordings.

The server owns sequence, period, phase, trial, and transition state. Research rows use a separate random subject identifier and allowlisted fields. Secrets, raw clicks, scene choices, actions, markers, passwords, recordings, IP addresses, full user-agent strings, and third-party telemetry are excluded. Export uses separate administrator credentials, bounded date/row limits, a purpose-separated pseudonym key, streaming delivery, and audit events.

Recruit up to 90 adults to retain at least 72 analyzable paired participants. Pilot participants are separate. Recruitment remains technically disabled until institutional fields, preregistration, ethics, data-protection, document approval, and verification gates are complete.
